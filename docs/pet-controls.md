# Dog and bone controls

The dog pauses for 25 seconds when petted. Tap it again or select the bone to wake it immediately. Only fetching, chewing, and the three-heart celebration temporarily disable the bone. Moving near the dog stops its movement so its button stays reachable.

Mouse users can pick up a bone, move it, and click to drop, or drag and release. Touch users can tap the bone to feed immediately or drag to place it. Keyboard activation feeds directly; Escape, pointer cancellation, and window blur cancel a carried bone. Changing portfolio sections clears pending pet timers and feeding state.
